# Payment provider assets and directory snapshots

The bank snapshot includes all 458 bank entries from the [BSP bank directory](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/SitePages/FinancialStability/DirBanksFIList.aspx), retrieved on 12 October 2026. It covers universal/commercial, thrift, rural/cooperative and digital banks. Only names, institution groups and public website addresses are stored. The issuer snapshot includes all 40 nonbank e-money issuers from the [BSP EMI list](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Lists/Directories/Attachments/7/emi.pdf), dated 31 May 2026. Familiar product names are matched to their issuer names; for example, GCash maps to G-Xchange and Maya Wallet stays separate from Maya Bank. MariBank also matches its previous SeaBank name.

These are dated directory snapshots. They do not imply every provider offers a retail wallet or every listed product is currently available. Users can create a custom source when a provider is missing. Bank entries use the bank-account source type by default; cards and e-wallets remain separate source types.

Logo assets come from each provider’s public website and are bundled locally, so choosing a source does not contact its provider. Logos remain the property of their respective owners. Assets were resized to a maximum of 96 pixels while preserving aspect ratio. Other providers use a generic bank or wallet icon with initials; those badges are not brand logos.

The featured logo sources below were checked on 11 October 2026. Blocked websites and missing image assets use the generic badge.
